1 · Your Mission

Your Snake game keeps the body as a list of positions. Every move, it must answer one question:

"Is the head's position anywhere in this list?"

The computer cannot look at the whole list at once. It can only compare one position at a time. So it checks them one by one, in order, until it finds a match — or runs out of list.

This is called linear search (or sequential search): linear because the work grows in a straight line with the size of the list.

7 · Bridge to Your Snake Game 🐍

Your Snake collision check is this activity:

  • The cards = the snake's body list. Each card is one body segment's position.
  • The ⭐ = the head's position. You're asking: "is the head anywhere in this body?"
  • Flipping one by one = the loop. for segment in body: if segment == head: crash!
  • Doubling = when your snake grows longer, collision checks take longer. A 40-segment snake costs twice the checks of a 20-segment one.
