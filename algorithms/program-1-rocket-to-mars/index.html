1 · Your Crew

In real programming teams, three jobs are separated. Today you will play all three — sometimes with Dad helping as the Bot.

👨‍💻 Developer

Writes the entire program first, before anything moves. No changes allowed mid-run!

🔍 Tester

Reads the instructions out loud, watches the Bot, and marks the exact line where a bug appears.

🤖 Bot

Follows the written instructions exactly — even if they are wrong. Never guesses what the Developer "meant".

2 · Your Programming Language

A programming language is just an agreement about what symbols mean. Ours has only three commands:

⬆️F — move Forward one square
↰L — turn Left 90° (stay in square)
↱R — turn Right 90° (stay in square)

Turning does not move you to a new square — you spin on the spot. This detail causes most bugs!

8 · Bridge to Your Snake Game 🐍

Everything you just did is inside your Snake game:

  • The Bot = the game loop. Every "tick", the loop reads the next instruction (move the head one square) and runs it — no help, no guessing.
  • The program = the snake's direction. Arrow keys change the program while it runs, exactly like the Developer handing new instructions to the Tester.
  • The grid = the game board. Rocket (1,5) → Mars (4,3) is the same idea as head (x,y) → food (x,y).
  • Debugging = fixing your game. When the snake goes the wrong way, you find the exact line — like the Tester underlining the bug.

Next steps on this path: queue (the snake's body), linear search (collision check), simulation (the full game loop).
