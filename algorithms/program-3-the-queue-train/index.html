💻 Program 3: The Queue Train 🚂

Alex's Algorithm Lab · Step 3 of the Snake-game algorithm path

Your snake's body is a queue: a new head car joins the front, the old tail car leaves the back. Miss one side and the whole train breaks!

Progress: 0 / 7 sections complete

1 · Your Mission

A queue is a line with one strict rule:

FIRST in → FIRST out.

New arrivals join the back. Departures leave from the front. Nobody cuts, nobody jumps.

Your Snake body works exactly like this. Every move:

body.insert(0, new_head)  # new head car joins the FRONT
body.pop()            # old tail car leaves the BACK

And when the snake eats? Skip the pop() — the tail stays, so the train grows by one car. 🚂

3 · Activity: Stack vs Queue 🥞

Don't mix these up! A stack (plates) is LAST in, first out. A queue (line) is FIRST in, first out. Try both with numbered items and watch the order.

🥞 Stack — push to top, pop from top

Popped order will be: 3, 2, 1…

🧍 Queue — join back, leave front

Served order will be: 1, 2, 3…

The snake's body is the queue one. The head is the front of the line — always served (moved) first.

7 · Bridge to Your Snake Game 🐍

  • body list = the train. Index 0 is the engine (head), the last index is the caboose (tail).
  • Every move = one queue cycle: new head joins the front, old tail leaves the back.
  • Eating = skip the pop(). That's the entire growing mechanism — one missing line!
  • Program 2 meets Program 3: after moving, the game runs a linear search (Program 2) over this queue to check collisions.
