🌿 2. How Words Grow

Three big ideas from your book:

➕ Add parts to a root

Other roots or word parts can be added to a root to grow new words. Example: scope (look at) + tele (from afar) = telescope.

🔤 The extra e / y

Often, when a word ends with a root, an e or y is added at the end, as in telephone and euphony. It doesn’t change the meaning — it just completes the word.

🌳 Roots can stand alone

Some roots can be a word all by themselves. Example: meter means measure — and it is also a word on its own!

Prefixes (beginnings) and suffixes (endings) join roots to make new words with new meanings.
