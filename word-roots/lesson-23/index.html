🛡️ 1. Meet the Prefixes & Roots

This lesson mixes 4 prefixes, 1 root, and 3 suffixes — all on the same team. The star of the show is serv, serve (to serve, protect, save).

PREFIX + ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
con- (with, together) + serv (to serve, protect, save) + -ation (an action or process) = conservation
The action of together protecting natural resources! 🛡️

Three examples straight from your book:

pre- + serve

= preserve — to protect before anything can harm it.

re- + serve

= reserve — to keep back or save for future use.

serv + -ant

= servant — one who is hired to serve.

💡 Tip: pre- protects early

pre- means before: you preserve strawberries before winter so you can enjoy them later!

pre- = before; in front of, as in preserve
re- = back, again, as in reserve
-ation = an action or process, as in conservation
