🪜 1. Meet the Root & Suffixes

A suffix is added to the end of a root. Notice how the tiny letter u connects grad to most of these suffixes!

ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
grad (step) + u + al (like, related to) = gradual
Say it slowly: a gradual climb goes up one small step at a time! 🪜

💡 Tip: What is a gradient?

“A gradient in color is when two or more colors blend together. Here is a simple example of a gradient, beginning with white and ending with black.”

See how the color changes in tiny steps? That’s exactly what grad (step) is about! 👀

🌈 Hands-On: Make a Real Rainbow!

You can make your own gradient using crayons or colored pencils. Or you can make a real rainbow! You will need these items:

  1. wide-mouthed, smooth, circular glass jar or large plain drinking glass filled with water
  2. small mirror
  3. flashlight
  1. Place the mirror inside the water-filled jar. Tilt the mirror slightly upward.
  2. In a very dark room with white walls, shine the flashlight onto the mirror. A rainbow appears! (Hint: If no rainbow appears at first, just change the angle of light from the flashlight or change the angle of the mirror.)

Can you explain why this happens? Check the answer key in the back of this book for the explanation. 🧪
