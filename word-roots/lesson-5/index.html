🤝 1. Meet the Prefixes & Root

From your book: four prefixes and one root. The root act means to do — the prefix tells you how it’s done!

PREFIX- + ROOT = NEW WORD
For example: inter- (between, among) + act (to do) = interact — to do things or talk with others!

counter- (against, opposite) + act

= counteract — to do or act against something.

re- (back, again) + act

= react — to respond to something by an action or show of feelings.

inter- (between, among) + act

= interact — to do things or talk with others.

trans- (through, across, beyond) + act

= transact — to do business with people or companies.

💥 Lesson 6 — Prefixes and Roots: ab-, dis-, e-, inter- + rupt

The root rupt means to break, burst — and the prefixes tell you how it breaks! ab- (away, from), dis- (apart, opposite of), e- (out, away, from), inter- (between, among).
