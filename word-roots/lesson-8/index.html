📍 1. Meet the Root & Suffixes

A root carries the core meaning. A suffix is added to the end of a root — and it changes or completes the meaning. This lesson has one root and four suffixes. Watch how just four suffixes turn one root into four words!

ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
loc (place) + -ate (to make, to act) = locate
The book’s footnote: A word can have more than one suffix. (locally has two!)

Four examples straight from your book:

loc + -ate

= locate — to find where something is.

loc + -al

= local — like or related to a particular place.

loc + -ale

= locale — a place or area where something special was, is, or happened.

loc + -al + -ly

= locally — in the manner of being local. Two suffixes in one word!
