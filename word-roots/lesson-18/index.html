🖐️ 1. Meet the Prefixes & Roots

This lesson mixes 3 prefixes, 1 root, and 3 suffixes — all on the same team. The star of the show is sens, sent (to feel, to perceive).

PREFIX + ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
dis- (apart, opposite of) + sent (to feel, to perceive) = dissent
Feeling apart from the crowd! 🖐️

Three examples straight from your book:

con- + sent

= consent — feeling with someone: giving permission.

in- + sens + it-ive

= insensitive — tending to not feel others' feelings.

sens + -ation

= sensation — the process of feeling something through the senses.

💡 Tip: in- means “not” here

In insensitive, in- means not — not feeling. Watch out: in- can also mean “in, into” in other lessons!

dis- = apart, opposite of, as in dissent
con- = with, together, as in consent
it-ive = tending to, as in sensitive
