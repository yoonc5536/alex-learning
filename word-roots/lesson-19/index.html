🧲 1. Meet the Prefixes & Root

This lesson mixes 3 prefixes, 1 root, and 2 suffixes — all on the same team. The star of the show is tract (to draw or pull, drag, draw out).

PREFIX + ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
sub- (under, below) + tract (to draw or pull) = subtract
To draw away to make something smaller — just like in math! 🧲

Three examples straight from your book:

at- + tract

= attract — to draw toward oneself, like a magnet.

ex- + tract

= extract — to draw out, like a tooth.

tract + -or

= tractor — that which pulls farm equipment.

💡 Tip: at- is “to, toward”

Think of a magnet: it pulls iron to itself → at- (to, toward) + tract (to draw or pull) = attract!

at- = to, toward, as in attract
ex- = out, away, from, as in extract
sub- = under, below, as in subtract
