🪞 1. Meet the Prefixes & Roots

This lesson mixes 2 prefixes, 1 root, and 3 suffixes — all on the same team. The star of the show is flect, flex (to bend).

PREFIX + ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
re- (back, again) + flect (to bend) + -ion (an action or process) = reflection
An image bent back at you from a mirror — or your thoughts bending back over something! 🪞

Three examples straight from your book:

flex + -ible

= flexible — able to bend or change.

in- + flex + -ible

= inflexible — not able to bend or change.

flex + -or

= flexor — that which bends: a muscle that bends a joint.

💡 Tip: re- bends two ways

The prefix re- means back, again. In reflection, light or thoughts bend back — a mirror image is light bending back at you!

re- = back, again, as in reflection
in- = not, as in inflexible
-or = one who, that which, as in flexor
