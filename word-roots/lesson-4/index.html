🚲 1. Meet the Prefixes & Root

From your book: three number prefixes and one very round root. Notice: in this lesson the prefix tells you how many!

PREFIX- + ROOT = NEW WORD
For example: uni- (one) + cycle = unicycle — a cycle with one wheel!

uni- (one) + cycle

= unicycle — a cycle with one wheel.

bi- (two) + cycle

= bicycle — a cycle with two wheels.

tri- (three) + cycle

= tricycle — a cycle with three wheels.

🤝 Lesson 5 — Prefixes and Roots: counter-, inter-, re-, trans- + act

The root act means to do — and this time the prefixes change how you do it! counter- (against, opposite), inter- (between, among), re- (back, again), trans- (through, across, beyond).

Coming words: counteract, react, interact, transact. People interact, antidotes counteract, and companies transact business!
