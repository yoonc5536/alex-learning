🌡️ 1. Meet the Roots

This lesson mixes 4 roots, and 1 suffix — all on the same team. The star of the show is therm, thermo, thermos (heat).

ROOT + ROOT = NEW WORD
thermo (heat) + meter (to measure) = thermometer
A heat-measurer! 🌡️

Three examples straight from your book:

therm + -al

= thermal — related to or producing heat.

thermo + stat

= thermostat — a heat-stayer: it keeps the temperature in position.

thermo + cline

= thermocline — a layer where the temperature bends (changes) sharply.

💡 Tip: thermo- words are everywhere

Once you know therm (heat), you'll spot it everywhere: thermometer (heat-measurer), thermostat (heat-stayer), even thermal underwear!

therm, thermo, thermos = heat
meter = to measure, as in thermometer
stat = to stay, position, as in thermostat
