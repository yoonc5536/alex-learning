🧩 1. Meet the Suffixes

From your book: “Now that you understand what a prefix is, learning about the other end of the word is easy! When letters are added to the end of a root, they’re called a suffix.”

ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
Just like PREFIX- + ROOT = NEW WORD — but the new part goes at the end!

act (to do) + -ive (tending to)

= active — being in physical motion; doing.

dent (tooth) + -ist (one who)

= dentist — one who works with or treats teeth.

ego (I, self) + -ism (state of being)

= egoism — state of being excessively self-interested.

Three suffixes to learn today:

💡 Tip: The most common suffixes

The most common suffixes are -s and -es. If there’s more than one cat, you add -s, and you have cats! If there is more than one class, you add -es and have classes.

🚲 Lesson 4 — Prefixes and Roots: uni-, bi-, tri-, cycle

Get ready to count! uni- means one, bi- means two, tri- means three — and cycle means circle. What do you get when you put them together? unicycle, bicycle, tricycle!

Bonus tip from the book: after uni-, bi-, tri- come quadr-, penta-, hexa-, hepta-, oct-, novem-, deca- (numbers 1–10) — then poly- (many) and omni- (all)!
