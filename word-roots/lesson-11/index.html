👂 1. Meet the Root & Suffixes

A suffix is added to the end of a root — and it changes the meaning of the word. Today’s root is audi, which means hear. Your book gives it seven different suffixes!

ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
audi (hear) + -ology (study of, science) = audiology

Three examples straight from your book:

audi + t + orium

= auditorium — a place where lectures, concerts, and other events are held.

audi + ence

= audience — a group of people listening to a performance.

audi + olog + ist

= audiologist — one who is trained to treat hearing and ear problems.

💡 Tip: Building with -olog and -ist

Watch how the parts stack up! audi + -ology = audiology (the scientific study of hearing). Add -ist — one who — and the person who knows that study is an audiologist: audi + olog + ist. Also notice that some words need a t before the suffix: audi + t + orium = auditorium, audi + t + ory = auditory.
