💥 1. Meet the Prefixes & Root

From your book: four prefixes and one root. The root rupt means to break, burst — the prefix tells you how it breaks!

PREFIX- + ROOT = NEW WORD
For example: e- (out) + rupt (to break, burst) = erupt — to break out of or burst from; explode!

dis- (apart) + rupt

= disrupt — to break apart or into an activity causing confusion or disorder.

e- (out) + rupt

= erupt — to break out of or burst from; explode.

inter- (between) + rupt

= interrupt — to come between or break into an activity or conversation.

ab- (away, from) + rupt

= abrupt — broken away or from something without warning.
