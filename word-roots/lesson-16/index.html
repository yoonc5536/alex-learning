🥛 1. Meet the Prefixes & Roots

This lesson mixes 2 prefixes, 1 root, and 2 suffixes — all on the same team. The star of the show is plen, plet, plete (full).

PREFIX + ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
com- (with, together) + plet (full) + -ion (an action or process) = completion
The process of coming fully together to an expected end! 🥛

Three examples straight from your book:

de- + plete

= deplete — to take away from fullness.

plen + t-y

= plenty — the state of having a full amount.

de- + plet + -ion

= depletion — the action of taking away from fullness.

💡 Tip: com- fills up, de- drains

com- (with, together) fills things up → complete. de- (from, away, down, apart) drains them → deplete. Opposite prefixes, opposite meanings!

com- = with, together, as in complete
de- = from, away, down, apart, as in deplete
t-y = state of, as in plenty
