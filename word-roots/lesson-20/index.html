🏗️ 1. Meet the Prefixes & Root

This lesson mixes 4 prefixes, 1 root, and 2 suffixes — all on the same team. The star of the show is struct (build).

PREFIX + ROOT + SUFFIX = NEW WORD
con- (with, together) + struct (build) = construct
To build things together! 🏗️

Three examples straight from your book:

in- + struct

= instruct — to build knowledge into someone's mind.

ob- + struct

= obstruct — to act against something being built.

in- + de- + struct + -ible

= indestructible — not able to be taken apart or destroyed.

💡 Tip: in- builds, de- destroys

in- (in, into) builds knowledge into minds → instruct. de- (down, apart) tears buildings down → destruct. Opposite prefixes again!

con- = with, together, as in construct
ob- = to, toward, against, as in obstruct
-ure = that which, as in structure
