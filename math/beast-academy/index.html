📄 The White-Paper Study Method

Reading is not learning. You only truly know something when you can write it from memory on a blank sheet of paper. Here is how every chapter on this page works:

1

👀 Study the map

Open the chapter mindmap. Read every branch and card until you understand each strategy — not just memorize it.

2

🙈 Hide the answers

Turn on White-Paper Mode on the mindmap page. Every example disappears — only the titles stay.

3

✍️ Write on blank paper

Grab a blank sheet. Write every strategy and its example from memory. No peeking — that is the whole point!

4

🔍 Check yourself

Click each card to reveal the answer. Compare with your paper and circle what you missed or got wrong.

5

🔄 Repeat until perfect

Try again another day. When you can write the whole map perfectly, tap ✓ Done on the chapter.
